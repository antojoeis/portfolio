Deep-learning AOI for LED inspection

Replacing manual checks of LED status on devices with a CNN classifier — trained on a GPU server, optimised with Intel OpenVINO and served in under 4 ms per image.

4 positive LED states to learn

All Blue
All Red
All Blue + SFP
All Red + SFP
<0.08%false-positive ratio
<4 msper image (OpenVINO)
>0.95confidence, all cases
SqueezeNetIntel OpenVINOModel OptimizerGPU training

Four experiments to get it right

Experiment I 450×450 · 500k iters

Couldn't separate pass/fail for the Red + SFP case.

Experiment II 450×450 · 1M iters

Longer training overfit — higher validation loss, weak test results.

Experiment III 227×227 · 120k iters · lr 0.006

Native input size helped, but false positives on fail cases stayed high.

Experiment IV ✓ 227×227 · 300k iters

Best checkpoint at 64k iterations: FPR 0.08%. Converted to OpenVINO IR (.xml/.bin) and verified on 143 mixed pass/fail images.

System design — from LED to verdict in under 4 ms
